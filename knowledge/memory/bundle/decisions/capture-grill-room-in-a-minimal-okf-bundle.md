# Capture Grill Room in a minimal OKF bundle before grilling more

Grill Room's knowledge lives in this bundle, which answers one question: what does Grill Room do today, for whom, and why. It follows the shape of `nworld-doctrine` and `slu-mw-demands` (OKF 0.2), in English, cut to what it needs now: fragments, a product module and a memory module.[^okf]

It grows only by need. A new type, module, tool or projection enters after the need has been debated and grilled; the root manifest lists what is deliberately not here yet.[^okf]

The first pass covers Pipe's meeting, Pipe's review and the founding spec, then projects one current-truth product spec. Grilling Pipe's pain points (bead `gr-2sw`) waits for it.[^okf]

Why: 16 feature specs holding 590 stories were written straight from grills, each a change on a description of the product that was never written; they name more than ten actors, and nothing states what Grill Room does now. In the owner's words: "we should start small, keep in mind YAGNI and when a new necesity comes we debate, grill it and build on top of it, we need to define the scope, define the knowledge and document it to take decisions".[^okf]
