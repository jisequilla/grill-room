# Readiness opens the interview

[Readiness](/interview/language/readiness.md) is part of Interview: judging whether an idea holds enough to grill is the interview's first move, and what the owner does about a not-ready idea is a question about the interview. It reads Project knowledge's [scout report](/project-knowledge/language/scout-report.md) when the session has a project, across the context map.[^contexts]

In this context the word is readiness; "preflight" is a word to avoid for it, because in Delivery a [pre-flight](/delivery/language/pre-flight.md) is the check before a ticket launches.[^contexts]

Why: its job is the interview's, and four of the Idea owner's partial or wanted use cases (the not-ready state, depth, technical level, staying oriented) then sit in one context. The owner accepted the three contexts with readiness here, over placing it elsewhere.[^contexts]
