# Setting a loose end aside offers two kinds

Setting a [loose end](/interview/language/loose-end.md) aside, besides out of scope, offers two kinds: an open question, which the owner still owes an answer to, and a [builder's call](/interview/language/builders-call.md), which goes to the builder and asks the owner nothing. The finish option for the second reads "Leave it to the builder". Open questions already stored stay owner-owed, and after finishing the owner can switch an item between the two kinds through the existing way of changing their mind, like any other change.[^ways]

Why: Pipe reads every "open question" as his to answer, when many are choices meant for the builder. Two kinds let the spec and the handoff ask only for what the owner owes.[^ways]
