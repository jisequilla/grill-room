# Sharpening

One of the three ways forward from a not-ready [readiness](/interview/language/readiness.md) verdict, offered as the button "Sharpen the idea". Its seed decisions are marked "from sharpening" when they enter the main grill. "Preflight" is a word to avoid for it, as it is for readiness.[^ways]
