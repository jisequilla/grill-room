# Technical level

A trait of the one [Idea owner](/product/roles/idea-owner.md), not a second role. Its three values separate how cards are worded (plain or technical) from who makes the technology calls (the owner or the builder). It is asked once, shown pre-filled at every session's setup step, and changeable at any round. "Persona" is a word to avoid for it: it does not split the role.[^depth]
