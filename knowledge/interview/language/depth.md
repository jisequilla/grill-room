# Depth

A value of the [session](session.md), asked at creation and changeable at any round. It decides which branches the interviewer opens and when it proposes being done, and readiness and the spec check scale their bar to it. "Scope" is a word to avoid for it, because a spec's scope is what it covers, not how far it was grilled.[^depth]
