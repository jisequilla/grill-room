# Builder's call

One of the ways to set a [loose end](/interview/language/loose-end.md) aside, with "Leave it to the builder" as its finish option. The spec lists it under "Left to the builder" and the briefs of the tickets it concerns list it under "Your call". "Open question" is a word to avoid for it: an open question is an answer the owner still owes.[^ways]
