# Pipe's review of an exported bundle

Pipe, an external tester, grilled a two-sided services marketplace in Grill Room and reviewed the bundle it exported: 23 tickets, 23 briefs, 13 waves, into a greenfield repository. The material is `docs/reviews/pipe-export-review-original.md` as received; `docs/reviews/pipe-export-review.md` maps its items to beads, and epic `gr-ibp` holds the fixes.

The decision trail, the briefs, the review protocol and the build records were judged strong. The export was not: it presented superseded decisions as live, passed raw answers through, kept a stale readiness verdict, and assumed facts about a repository that had none.

This is the only first-hand account of someone other than the owner using Grill Room end to end.
