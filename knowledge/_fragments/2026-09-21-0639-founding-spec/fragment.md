# Founding spec of Grill Room

The spec as committed in `b33ca30` ("Scaffold grill-room, install grilling skills, publish spec and tickets"), then at `.scratch/grill-room/spec.md`. The live copy at `.grill-room/grill-room/spec.md` has been edited since; this is the version the app was first built from.

It frames Grill Room as the owner's own tool: the grilling interview works, but a chat window is a poor container for it. Rounds arrive as walls of numbered questions, the design tree is invisible, changed answers do not flag what they undermine, nothing can be parked or pushed back on reliably, sessions cannot be resumed, and no spec or tickets come out without manual steps. The answer is a local, single-user app that runs the interview as a structured workspace.
