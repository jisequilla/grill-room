# Builder's calls have their own spec section and reach the tickets they concern

The [spec](/delivery/language/spec.md) gets an app-added section, `## Left to the builder`, after Further Notes, and the spec section check enforces it when any [builder's call](/interview/language/builders-call.md) exists. Open questions the owner owes stay in Further Notes. Breaking into [tickets](/delivery/language/ticket.md) attaches each builder's call to the tickets it concerns, and their [briefs](/delivery/language/brief.md) list it under "Your call". The [handoff](/delivery/language/handoff.md) section "Questions the spec and tickets leave open" keeps only the owner-owed ones.[^ways]

Why: a builder needs the choices left to it next to the ticket it is building, and the owner should be asked only for what is theirs to answer. The owner chose a section of its own over a marker inside Further Notes.[^ways]
