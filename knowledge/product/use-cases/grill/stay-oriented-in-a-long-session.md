# Stay oriented in a long session

A grill of 36 rounds and more than 40 questions tires the owner. Pipe wants a recap at the end of each phase ("here is what we agreed, continue?") and to lock a finished phase so the next part branches off it.[^meeting]

**Technical level.** Fatigue comes sooner for an owner who has to work harder to follow each question.

**Missing.** No recap and no notion of a phase exist in the app or the interviewer's instructions.
